OB Theory & Research Foundations

Why The Mutual Works: Scientific Foundations

Grounded in peer-reviewed research on psychological safety, contracting schemas, goal setting, and cognitive alignment.

Dr. Amy Edmondson (Harvard Business School) Psychological Safety & Voice Climate

Subordinates routinely self-censor during ambiguous briefings due to impression management (fear of looking incompetent in high power-distance cultures). The Ambiguity Interceptor provides psychologically safe conversational scripts that depersonalize the inquiry and frame clarification as proactive stewardship.

Dr. Denise Rousseau (Carnegie Mellon University) Psychological Contract Theory & Relational Schemas

Unwritten mental models between manager and employee frequently diverge. The Micro Contract externalizes implicit assumptions into an explicit mutual agreement before work starts, preventing perceived psychological contract breach and toxic workplace disillusionment.

Dr. Edwin Locke & Dr. Gary Latham Goal-Setting Theory & Specificity Effects

Specific, difficult, and bounded goals produce significantly higher performance than 'do your best' or vague managerial guidance like 'make it pop'. The Handshake enforces crisp definition-of-done criteria that convert aesthetic subjectivity into measurable deliverables.

Daniel Kahneman & Amos Tversky The Planning Fallacy & Ambiguity Aversion

Delegators consistently underestimate project complexity when communicating casually ('adjust maadi', 'sometime next week only'). The Out-of-Scope clause actively blocks boundary erosion and creeping expectations by making anti-goals as explicit as deliverables.

Dr. Karl Weick (University of Michigan) Organizational Sensemaking & Enacted Environments

Organizational members make sense of ambiguous events retrospectively. When delegations lack clear bounds, individuals construct divergent narratives that collide during performance reviews. Real-time interception aligns sensemaking concurrently during the briefing.

Dr. Edgar Schein (MIT Sloan School of Management) Process Consultation & Power-Distance Leveling

Addressing power asymmetry requires structural intervention rather than personal bravery. The Mutual acts as a neutral third-party instrument that levels power dynamics, enabling junior executors to hold senior leaders to explicit turnaround SLAs without insubordination risk.

SECTION 1. DATA CONTROLLER & SCOPE

1.1 Identity of Controller. This Global Privacy Policy ("Privacy Policy") governs the processing of personal data by The Mutual Technologies, Inc. ("The Mutual", "we", "us", or "our"), functioning as the Data Controller under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation, "GDPR"), the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018 as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act ("CCPA/CPRA"), and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act", India).

1.2 Scope of Operations. This Policy applies to all interactions with our browser extensions (Chrome, Safari, Edge), native desktop sidecar applications, macOS menu bar utilities, local Python daemon servers, and web companion interfaces.

SECTION 2. CATEGORIES OF DATA COLLECTED

2.1 Audio Waveform Streams (Ephemeral). When you enable live microphone capture, raw acoustic waveforms are captured through device sensor APIs. Under our default local architecture, raw audio buffers are processed in temporary RAM (chunks < 1000 milliseconds) and are immediately discarded following tokenization into text. Raw audio is never persistently written to permanent disk storage.

2.2 Text Transcripts & Meeting Notes. Text representations of spoken dialogue are retained in client-side application memory (`state.capturedTranscript`) for the active duration of your meeting session. Transcripts are automatically purged upon session termination or when you click "Clear".

2.3 Account & Profile Metadata. User-provided identifiers including full name, work email address, organizational role, designated manager identity, and SLA preferences.

2.4 API Credentials & Security Tokens. Third-party AI model API keys (e.g., Google Gemini API keys) are stored solely within your browser's sandboxed local storage (`chrome.storage.local` / `localStorage`) and are never routed to The Mutual's central servers.

SECTION 3. LOCAL ON-DEVICE VS. CLOUD PROCESSING ARCHITECTURE

3.1 Local Whisper Large-v3-Turbo Engine. By default, The Mutual utilizes a local instance of the OpenAI Whisper Large-v3-Turbo model (809M parameters) hosted on the user's host machine via Apple Silicon Metal Performance Shaders (MPS) and CoreML. In this operating mode:

(a) Acoustic audio is processed entirely within local hardware RAM;
(b) Zero audio bytes, phonemes, or voice biometrics are transmitted over the public Internet;
(c) Transcription operates with complete offline resilience; and
(d) No third-party cloud vendor has access to the raw acoustic conversation.

3.2 Cloud Inference Sub-Processors. When you explicitly configure cloud AI processing (e.g., Google Gemini 1.5/2.0 Flash or OpenAI GPT-4o for contract generation), textual transcript excerpts are transmitted over TLS 1.3 encrypted connections directly to the selected API provider using your configured API key. All transmissions are subject to the enterprise Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) of those respective providers, which strictly prohibit utilizing customer inputs for foundation model retraining.

SECTION 4. LEGAL BASES FOR PROCESSING UNDER GDPR & DPDP ACT

We process personal data strictly in reliance upon recognized lawful grounds under GDPR Article 6 and the Indian DPDP Act 2023:

(a) Explicit Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): Audio sensor activation occurs only upon your affirmative manual trigger ("Start Live Listening"). Consent may be revoked instantaneously at any moment by clicking "Stop";

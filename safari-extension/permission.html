Enable Microphone Access

To transcribe delegation briefings and detect ambiguous directives in real-time, The Mutual needs microphone authorization in Chrome.

Click the button above to authorize your microphone.
⚠️ If clicking "Allow" shows an error or does nothing:
  1. Check Chrome URL Bar: Look at the address bar at the very top of this tab. Click the 🎛️ Tune / Site Settings or 🔒 Lock icon immediately to the left of chrome-extension://.... Ensure Microphone is set to Allow (not Block).
  2. Check macOS System Settings: Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and make sure Google Chrome is toggled ON.
  3. Alternative 1 (Web Companion): You can run the entire platform with full native mic access at http://localhost:8000 (no extension permission hurdles).
  4. Alternative 2 (Google Meet): In Google Meet, simply click CC (Closed Captions). The Mutual captures Meet captions directly with zero mic permissions needed!
